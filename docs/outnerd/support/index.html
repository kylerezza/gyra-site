Outnerd support

Need help? Email support@gyra.io. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Include your username (in Me), your iPhone model and iOS version, and what happened and roughly when. A screenshot helps. Never send card details.

Getting started

Why does Outnerd ask for my birth year?

Outnerd is for ages 13 and up, and some features work differently for players under 18.

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can play as a guest right away. To keep your progress, tap Save your win and sign up with Apple, Google or email. You need an account to send challenges, join friend leagues, add friends, choose your own name and get Pro.

I signed in and my guest progress is gone.

If you signed in to an account you already had, Outnerd opens that account, and the guest progress on that device isn't added to it.

Duels

How many duels can I play a day?

The Duel tab shows how many you have left. Free players get 5 a day and Pro players get 30. Duels come back at midnight on your own clock. Your practice duel, placement duels and league pack quizzes don't use them.

Who am I playing against?

Duels aren't live. Your rival is a recorded run on the same questions: a friend's or league-mate's, another player's, or a pacer we built to match your level. Tap How it works on the Duel tab for more.

I left a duel. Why did I lose?

Quitting a duel, or leaving the app closed too long in the middle of one, counts as a forfeit.

I think an answer is wrong.

Tap Report question on the duel result, or email us the question and the answer you expected. We check every report.

Challenges and leagues

How do I challenge a friend?

On the Duel tab, open Challenges and tap Challenge a friend, or send your run from a duel result. Share the 6-character code. Your friend enters it under Challenges › Have a code? and races your run. Challenges expire after the time shown in the app.

My code doesn't work.

Check the 6 characters: codes never use 0, O, 1 or I. The challenge may have ended, or the league may be full.

How do friend leagues and league packs work?

Start a friend league from the Leagues tab and share its code. Every duel and challenge your members play adds to one table, and it never resets. League packs are themed sets of 10 quizzes you race with friends on their own table. One pack is free; others are in the Shop.

Outnerd Pro and credits

What is Pro?

A monthly subscription with more daily duels, more and bigger friend leagues, bigger league pack groups, the Pro mark and a rare item each month. The Pro screen shows the price in your currency.

How do I cancel Pro?

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Outnerd › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app or your Outnerd account doesn't cancel it.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose the Outnerd purchase. If Apple refunds a credit pack, those credits are taken back; your account isn't penalised.

I paid but don't have Pro or my credits.

Use Restore Pro subscription in Me › Settings, or Restore purchases in the Shop, while signed in to the account you bought on. If that doesn't help, email us your username and the purchase date.

Do credits expire?

No. Credits you buy never expire. Everything in the Shop shows its price before you confirm, and nothing paid is random.

Notifications

Change them in Me › Settings › Notifications or iOS Settings › Notifications › Outnerd. Players under 18 don't get reminder or "come back" notifications.

Safety

Someone has an offensive name or is cheating.

Tap their name or avatar, then Report. We review every report.

How do I block someone?

Tap their name or avatar and choose Block. They won't be served as your rival, can't challenge you and can't join your friend league. They aren't told. You can unblock them in Settings.

My account was banned.

If you think it's a mistake, appeal in the app or email support@gyra.io with your username and why. We'll review it and reply.

Your account and data

How do I delete my account?

Go to Me › Settings › Delete account and confirm. This erases your profile, your game history and any bought credits left on the account. If you signed in with Apple, Outnerd's access to your Apple ID is revoked. Cancel any Pro subscription first.

Can I get a copy of my data, or correct it?

Email support@gyra.io from the email on your account, or include your username. See the Outnerd privacy policy for your rights.

How do I turn off usage analytics?

Go to Me › Settings › Privacy Choices.

Parents

Outnerd is for ages 13 and up. If you think a child under 13 is using it, email support@gyra.io and we'll delete the account.
