gyra  (guy·ruh)  n.  the deadliest cat.

Small. Precise. Rarely misses.

gyra is a one-person studio making small, finished apps for iPhone. Each one is stalked for a long time and released only when it lands.

She’s on her way.

The black-footed cat is Africa’s smallest wild cat, and its most successful hunter: about 6 hunts in 10 end in a catch. Lions manage about 1 in 4.

How she hunts

Three rules from a very small cat.

01

Stay small.

One person, a few apps, each doing one thing well. Small is how you get close.

02

Stalk first.

Most of the work happens before anyone sees it. Ideas are watched for a long time before the pounce.

03

Land it.

Nothing ships half-caught. If it isn’t right yet, it waits.
