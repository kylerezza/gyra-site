Outnerd community rules

Effective date: 23 November 2026

These rules apply to everyone who plays Outnerd. Together with Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement they are the terms of use for the app. By continuing past the first screen, you agree to them. Our privacy policy explains how we handle your data.

Play fair, play nice

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive players. Don't:

  • use a name, @username or league name that is hateful, sexual, violent, harassing, or that pretends to be Outnerd, gyra, staff or another person;
  • harass, threaten or bully other players, or share anyone's personal information;
  • cheat: bots, scripts, modified apps, sharing answers during a duel, or exploiting bugs;
  • create accounts to get around a ban, a limit or an invite reward, or sell or trade accounts or items;
  • use Outnerd for anything illegal.

Reports and what happens next

Tap a player's name or avatar to Report or Block them, or tap Report question on a duel result. Blocked players are never served as your rival and can't join your league. They aren't told.

We aim to review every report within 24 hours. Depending on what happened, we may hide or reset a name, remove a league name, give a warning, suspend an account for 24 hours, or ban it. When several separate reports about a player arrive in a short time, the app may act automatically; a person reviews every automatic ban and can reverse it. If we act against your account, the app tells you what we did and why.

If you think we got it wrong, appeal in the app or email support@gyra.io with your @username. A person reviews it again and replies.

Your rivals

Duels aren't live. Every rival is a recorded run on the same questions: a friend's, a league-mate's, another player's, or a pacer we built to match your level. League pack pacers are shown as The Nerd.

Credits, Pro and the Shop

  • Credits are a licence to use inside Outnerd, not money or property. They don't expire, can't be cashed out, transferred or traded, and end if your account is deleted.
  • Every Shop item shows its price in credits and your balance after, before you confirm. Nothing paid is random.
  • Apple takes every payment and handles refunds. If Apple refunds a credit pack, those credits are taken back; your account isn't penalised for a refund.
  • Outnerd Pro is a monthly subscription that renews until you cancel it in iOS Settings. The Pro screen shows what it includes and its price.
  • Leagues, league packs and seasons award badges, titles and frames only. They are never sold, never transferable and have no cash value. Apple is not a sponsor of, and is not involved in, any Outnerd league or competition.

Changes and contact

We may update these rules. If a change matters, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. Questions, reports from outside the app, and requests from authorities go to support@gyra.io (English).
