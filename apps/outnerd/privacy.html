Outnerd Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

Thanks for playing Outnerd! This Privacy Policy explains:

  • what personal data we collect about you and why;
  • how we use and share it; and
  • the choices and rights you have.

This Privacy Policy covers the Outnerd app for iPhone and its related pages on gyra.io (together, the "Service"). We may update it from time to time by posting a new version at https://gyra.io/outnerd/privacy/. If we make a material change, we will tell you as the law requires, including with a notice in the app before the change takes effect. If you keep using the Service after the new version takes effect, the new version applies.

Contact us

For questions about your data, or to make a request about it, email us. Please include your @username so we can find your account.

  • Email: support@gyra.io

The data we collect

What we collect depends on how you use the Service and what the law allows.

Data you give us

  • Your birth year, which the app asks for the first time you open it.
  • Sign-in details if you create an account with Apple, Google or email: your email address and, if the provider shares it, your name. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we receive only Apple's relay address.
  • Your player profile: display name, @username, avatar and the cosmetics you equip.
  • Your friend list and the friend requests you send and receive.
  • Reports you make about other players or questions (including any text you add), the players you block, and the text of any ban appeal.
  • Messages you send to our support.

We never receive your payment card or bank details. Apple takes every payment.

Data we collect automatically

  • Data about your account and progress, including an internal account ID that we create automatically (guests get one too).
  • Your gameplay: the questions you get, your answers and how fast you give them, your scores and results, your Rating and tier, level, badges, league tables and points, challenges, head-to-head records, and the items and credits you own. Each duel you play is recorded so that other players can race it later.
  • Your purchases: which Pro subscription or credit pack you bought, transaction IDs, dates, renewals, cancellations and refunds.
  • Device data: device model, iOS and app version, language, locale and region setting, time zone, and the browser engine details (user agent) the app reports.
  • A push notification token if you allow notifications, and a random analytics ID stored on your device.
  • Your IP address, which we store only in hashed (scrambled) form: the latest one and up to 20 earlier ones.
  • Security checks from Apple's App Attest (through Firebase App Check) that confirm requests come from the genuine app on a real device.
  • How you use the Service: which screens you open and which buttons you tap, from a fixed list of in-app events, plus error reports when something breaks.
  • Codes from your clipboard, only when you tap Paste code and allow it, and only to find a 6-character Outnerd code.

Data we get from partners

  • From Apple, through RevenueCat: confirmation of your purchases, renewals and refunds.
  • From Apple Search Ads, through RevenueCat and Apple's AdServices framework: which ad campaign (if any) led to your install. This does not use your advertising identifier.
  • From Apple or Google when you sign in with them: your email address and, if shared, your name.

Why we collect your data

To make the Service work

To provide the Service under our agreement with you, we use your data to:

  • create your account or guest profile and let you play;
  • run duels, matchmaking, Ratings, leagues, league packs, challenges and friends;
  • verify purchases and deliver Pro and credits, and keep your credit wallet;
  • send you notifications about the Service that you have allowed; and
  • answer your questions and support requests.

To make the Service better for players

We have a legitimate interest in using the data we need to:

  • understand how the Service is used and improve it (usage analytics);
  • find and fix bugs (error reports);
  • learn which campaigns bring new players (install attribution); and
  • develop new features and content.

To keep the Service safe and fair

We have a legitimate interest in using the data we need to:

  • detect and stop cheating, fraud, refund abuse, multi-account abuse and ban evasion;
  • review reports and blocks, and moderate player names; and
  • act against players who break the rules.

Some of these actions are automatic. When several separate reports about a player arrive in a short time, the app can give a warning, then a 24-hour suspension, then a permanent ban. A person reviews every automatic ban and can reverse it, and you can appeal any ban in the app.

To apply the rules for your age

We use your birth year to apply the protections for players under 18 (see "Children and teens"). This is based on our legal obligations and our legitimate interest in protecting young players.

To analyze and segment

For all of the purposes above, we may analyze and group the data we hold, for example to match you with rivals of a similar Rating.

With your consent

We send push notifications only after you allow them in iOS.

What we don't do

Outnerd has no ads. We don't sell your personal data, we don't share it for targeted advertising, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites. We don't collect your advertising identifier (IDFA), your precise location, your contacts, your photos or your microphone.

Where the law in your country doesn't let us rely on legitimate interests for something above, we use another lawful basis that it allows, such as your consent.

Who can see your data

Besides gyra, your data can be seen in these cases:

Other players

Outnerd is a social game. Other players can see your display name, @username, avatar and cosmetics, Rating tier, level, badges and Pro mark, your place and points in leagues and on leaderboards, and your results and head-to-head records against them. Your recorded duels are replayed as rivals for other players, who see your display name and your result. Other players never see your email, birth year, purchases or device data.

Our service providers

These companies help us run the Service. They process your data only on our instructions and under contracts that limit what they may do with it:

  • Google (Firebase and Google Cloud, United States): sign-in, database, servers, push notifications, App Check and hosting.
  • RevenueCat (United States): subscriptions and credit purchases, and Apple Search Ads attribution.
  • Apple: App Store payments, Sign in with Apple, push delivery and App Attest.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google.

Other companies and public authorities

We may share data to verify payments and fight fraud and illegal activity, to answer lawful requests from public authorities, to comply with the law, or to protect the rights, property or safety of gyra, our players or others. If the Service is sold or transferred, your data may go to the new owner, who must protect it as this policy describes.

International data transfers

Our servers are in the United States, so your data will be transferred outside the country where you live. Where the law requires it (for example for data from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland), we use recognised safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK Addendum, or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified under it.

Your rights and options

Push notifications

You choose which notifications you get in Me → Settings → Notifications, and you can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings.

Usage analytics

Usage analytics are on by default. You can turn them off at any time in Me → Settings → Privacy Choices. Error reports and security data are not covered by this switch, because we need them to keep the Service working and safe.

Delete your account

Go to Me → Settings → Delete account. This works for guests too. We delete your profile, game data, recorded duels and analytics events, revoke Outnerd's access to your Apple ID if you signed in with Apple, and delete your customer record at RevenueCat. Credits left in your wallet are lost. Deleting your account does not cancel Pro: Apple bills it, so cancel it in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Access your data

If you ask, we will send you a copy of your personal data in an electronic format.

Your other rights

You can also ask us to correct your data, delete it, restrict how we use it, or send it to you in a portable format. You can object to how we use it, including for analytics and attribution, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. You can ask for a person to review an automated decision about you.

To use any of these rights, email support@gyra.io (see "Contact us"). To protect your account, we may ask you to confirm it's you, for example by writing from the email on your account or answering questions about your account. Once you have confirmed it, you may name an authorised agent to act for you. We reply within the time the law sets: one month in the EEA and UK, 45 days in the U.S. We will not treat you differently for using your rights.

If you're not happy with our answer, you can complain to your local data protection authority (in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK, where the UK's is the Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk).

Cookies and similar technologies

The app doesn't use cookies. It stores a random analytics ID and your settings on your device so the Service works and remembers your choices. Our analytics are off when you turn off Privacy Choices. The gyra.io website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising.

How we protect your data

Security

We protect your data with encrypted connections, access controls, App Check device checks, rate limits and restricted, logged admin access, and we keep improving these measures. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account exists or as long as we need it to provide the Service, and then delete or de-identify it. Specifically:

  • Usage analytics events: 90 days.
  • Error reports: 14 days (app) or 60 days (server).
  • Individual duel records: about 12 weeks after the Rating season they belong to ends.
  • Purchase and credit records: 7 years, for tax and accounting. After you delete your account, your account ID on them is replaced with a code.
  • The hashed email of a permanently banned account: as long as the ban stands, so it can't be dodged with a new account.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.

If you ask us to delete your data, we may still keep what we need to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our rules. Deleted data may stay in backups for a limited time until they are overwritten.

Children and teens

Protecting young players matters to us. Outnerd is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them. If we learn that a child under 13 has given us personal data, we delete it and close the account. Parents and guardians can contact us at support@gyra.io to review, change or delete their child's data.

Players we know to be 13 to 17 get a more limited experience. A player with no birth year on record is treated as under 18.

  • No reminder, come-back or "someone passed you" notifications.
  • Other notifications (such as challenges and league results) only if turned on, and never at night.
  • They can only be found in friend search by their exact @username.
  • They can block and report other players at any time.

Purchases go through Apple, so Family Sharing's Ask to Buy and Screen Time controls apply.

For residents of certain U.S. states

Residents of California and other U.S. states with privacy laws (including Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas and Oregon) have the rights to know, access, correct, delete and port their personal data, to opt out of sale, targeted advertising and significant profiling, and to not be discriminated against. We give these rights to all U.S. players.

  • We do not sell or share personal data for cross-context behavioural advertising, and haven't in the last 12 months. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the data of anyone under 16.
  • We use sensitive personal data (your account login) only to sign you in.
  • If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. If you're still not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general.
  • Because we don't sell or share, Global Privacy Control has nothing to opt you out of. If that changes, we will honour it as an opt-out.

Categories of personal data we collect, in California's terms: identifiers (account ID, email, @username, push token, analytics ID, hashed IP); customer records (name and email); protected characteristics (age, from birth year); commercial information (purchase history); internet or electronic activity (gameplay, in-app events, error reports); inferences (Rating and matchmaking estimates); and sensitive personal data (account login). Sources, purposes, recipients and retention are described above.
