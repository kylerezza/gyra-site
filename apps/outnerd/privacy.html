Outnerd Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Outnerd app collects, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights and choices you have. Outnerd is a 1v1 trivia duel game for iPhone.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run the game: your account, your duels, your rankings, your purchases, and some device and safety data.
  • Outnerd has no ads. We don't sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising, and we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We don't collect your precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Usage analytics are on by default. You can turn them off in Me → Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • You can delete your account in the app at any time: Me → Settings → Delete account.

1. Who we are

Outnerd is made by gyra, the independent developer of Outnerd ("gyra", "we", "us"). gyra is the trading name of the individual developer shown as the seller on Outnerd's App Store page. gyra decides how your personal information is used, so gyra is the "controller" under data protection law.

Contact for anything in this policy: support@gyra.io. Support page: https://gyra.io/outnerd/support/

2. Who can use Outnerd

Outnerd is for people 13 and older, in every country where it's on the App Store. Players aged 13 to 17 get extra protections (section 12).

3. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Age: your birth year, asked the first time you open the app. We use it to apply the right rules for your age.
  • Account: if you sign in with Apple, Google or email, we receive your email address and, where the provider shares it, your name. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only get Apple's relay address.
  • Profile: your display name, @username, avatar and the cosmetics you equip.
  • Friends: your friend list and the friend requests you send and receive.
  • Reports and appeals: reports you make about other players (including any text you add), blocks, and the text of any ban appeal.
  • Messages to us: emails you send to support and our replies.

Information created when you play

  • Duels and runs: the questions you got, your answers, how fast you answered, your score and the result. Each run is recorded so other players can race it later (section 5).
  • Progress: your Rating and tier, level and XP, badges, Rating season results, league standings and points, challenges you send or answer, head-to-head records and the items you own.
  • Seen questions: which questions you've already had, so they don't repeat too soon.

Purchases

Apple handles every payment. We never see your card or bank details. Through RevenueCat, Apple tells us what you bought (Outnerd Pro or a credit pack), transaction IDs, dates, renewals, cancellations and refunds. We keep a credit ledger (credits bought, earned, spent and refunded) and a list of the items you own.

Device and technical information

  • Identifiers: an account ID that we create for you (guests get one too), a push notification token if you allow notifications, and a random analytics ID stored on your device.
  • Settings: language, locale and country (from your device settings), time zone and notification preferences.
  • Security signals: Apple's App Attest checks, used through Firebase App Check, which confirm that requests come from the real Outnerd app on a real device.
  • Hashed IP address: a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address, not the address itself. We keep the latest one plus up to 20 earlier ones to fight cheating, multi-account abuse and ban evasion.
  • Error reports: when something breaks, the app sends an error message, the screen it happened on, your device type, app version and browser engine details (user agent), linked to your account ID.

How you use the app (usage analytics)

We record which screens you open and which buttons you tap, from a fixed list of in-app events. These events are stored with your analytics ID and your account ID. They never include what you type, your birth year or an advertising identifier. Analytics are on by default, and you can switch them off in Me → Settings → Privacy Choices. Error reports and security data are not covered by this switch, because we need them to keep the app working and safe.

How you found Outnerd

When you first open the app, we record where you came from if we can tell (for example the App Store, an Apple Search Ads campaign, or a link or code another player shared). For Apple Search Ads, RevenueCat collects Apple's attribution token through Apple's AdServices framework. This tells us which campaign led to the install. It doesn't use your advertising identifier and doesn't track you across other apps. When you share a result or challenge, we record what kind of share it was, not who you sent it to.

Codes from your clipboard

When you tap Paste code, iOS asks your permission. The app then reads the clipboard only to find a 6-character Outnerd code.

Do you have to give us this information?

You need to give your birth year to play, and sign-in details to create an account. Everything else comes from using the app. You can play without an account as a guest. Guests can do most things, but need an account to create or join friend leagues, send challenges or pick a custom name.

Automated decisions

  • Matchmaking: your Rating, worked out from your results, decides which runs you race.
  • Report-based actions: the app takes some actions automatically when several separate reports about a player arrive in a short time. In order: a warning, then a 24-hour suspension, then a permanent ban.
  • Review and appeal: a person reviews every automatic ban and can reverse it. You can appeal any ban in the app and ask for a person to review the decision.

5. What other players can see

  • Your display name, @username, avatar and cosmetics, Rating tier, level, badges, and the Pro mark if you have Pro.
  • Your place and points in leagues and on leaderboards, and your results and head-to-head records against them.
  • Your recorded runs. Your answers and timing on a set of questions can be replayed as a rival for other players. They see your display name and your result on that run.

Other players never see your email, birth year, purchase details or device information. Players under 18 can only be found in friend search by their exact @username.

7. International transfers

Our servers are in the United States, and we and our providers may access your information from other countries. When personal information from the EEA, the UK or Switzerland goes to a country without an adequacy decision, we rely on recognised safeguards. These are the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum), or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified under it. You can ask us for a copy of the relevant safeguards.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections, access controls, App Check device checks, rate limits and logged, restricted admin access. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

10. Your choices

  • Notifications: choose which kinds you get in Me → Settings → Notifications, or turn them off in iOS Settings.
  • Usage analytics: switch off in Me → Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Blocking: a blocked player is never matched against you, can't challenge you and can't join your friend league.
  • Profile: change your name, avatar and cosmetics in the app.
  • Delete your account: go to Me → Settings → Delete account. This works for guests too. We delete your profile, game data, recorded runs and analytics events. If you signed in with Apple, we revoke Outnerd's access to your Apple ID. We also delete your customer record at RevenueCat. Open challenges are withdrawn, and your place in a league is handed to an unnamed filler. Credits left in your wallet are lost. Deleting your account does not cancel Pro. Apple bills you, so cancel in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

11. Your privacy rights

You can make any request below by emailing support@gyra.io from the email linked to your account, or by including your @username. We may ask for information to confirm it's you. We won't charge you, and we won't treat you differently for using your rights.

EEA, UK and Switzerland

You have the right to:

  • access your personal information and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete it (you can also do this in the app);
  • restrict how we use it;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, including analytics and attribution;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • ask for a person to review an automated decision about you.

We reply within one month (this can be extended by two more months for complex requests, and we'll tell you if so). If you complain to us, we'll acknowledge it within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, or in the UK to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

United States

Residents of California and of other states with comprehensive privacy laws (including Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, Utah, Texas, Oregon and others) have rights to know and access, correct, delete and port their personal information. They also have the right to opt out of sale, targeted advertising and significant profiling, and the right not to be discriminated against. We offer these rights to all US users.

  • We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and have not done so in the past 12 months. We have no actual knowledge of selling or sharing the information of anyone under 16.
  • We use sensitive personal information (your account login) only to sign you in.
  • Authorised agents can make a request for you with your signed permission.
  • Response times: we reply within 45 days, extendable once by another 45 days.
  • Appeals: if we decline your request, reply to our answer to appeal. If you're still not satisfied, you can contact your state attorney general.
  • Global Privacy Control: because we don't sell or share, there's nothing to opt out of. If that ever changes, we'll treat GPC as an opt-out.
  • Do Not Track: we don't track you across other services.
  • California "Shine the Light": we don't give personal information to others for their direct marketing.

12. Children and teens

Under 13: Outnerd is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that a child under 13 is using Outnerd, we delete the account. If you think one is, email support@gyra.io.

13 to 17: players we know to be under 18 get these protections. A player with no birth year on record is treated as under 18.

  • No reminder, come-back or "someone passed you" notifications.
  • Other notifications (such as league results and challenges) only if turned on, and never at night.
  • Found in friend search only by their exact @username.
  • They can block and report other players at any time.

In-app purchases go through Apple, so Family Sharing's Ask to Buy and Screen Time controls apply. Parents and guardians can contact support@gyra.io with any questions.
