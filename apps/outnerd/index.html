Outnerd

A 1v1 trivia duel for iPhone, by gyra.

Seven questions, one 70-second clock, the same deck for you and your rival. Right answers score, fast answers score more. Every rival is a recorded run, so you're racing in seconds: a friend's, a league-mate's, another player's, or a pacer built to match your level.

Send your run to a friend as a 6-character code, start a league with your crew, race themed league packs, and climb from Bronze to Legend.
