Stitchwell

A calm cross stitch game for iPhone and iPad, by gyra.

Real two-leg stitches on cream linen, one square at a time. Pick a colour, tap the cloth, and watch a pattern come together. A new Daily Hoop every day, hundreds of patterns from flowers to public-domain paintings, and a Wall for everything you finish.

Turn your own photo into a pattern, right on your device. No ads and no account.
