Stitchwell Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Stitchwell for iPhone and iPad (the "app") collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and what rights you have. It applies only to the app. The gyra.io website has its own privacy page.

Summary of key points

  • No account. You do not need to give us your name or email to use the app.
  • Ads in the free version. From version 1.4, if you do not have a plan, the app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects device and usage data to show and measure them. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first, and where the law gives you that choice you can change it at any time in the app (Settings › Purchases › Privacy choices) or by emailing us. Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you open the app with an active plan, the ad software isn't started and you see no ads.
  • Usage statistics. The app can send statistics about how it is used to our analytics provider. You can switch this off at any time.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data, except that ads in the free version may count as a "sale" or "sharing" under some US state laws. You can opt out (section 10).

2. What data we collect

Usage statistics (collected automatically, if sharing is on)

When "Share usage analytics" is on, the app sends events about how it is used, for example:

  • patterns started, left and finished (the pattern's catalogue number, size and how far along it is), and how many patterns you have finished;
  • the subscription screen: when it is shown, which plan is chosen, and whether a free trial or purchase went through, renewed, was cancelled or was refunded;
  • steps of making a photo pattern, such as "photo loaded" or "pattern made", with the pattern's size in stitches and number of colours;
  • taps such as opening a notification, sharing a finished piece and which export you shared;
  • in the free version, the ads shown to you: their type, where in the app, and the revenue Google reports for them;
  • time spent in a pattern and your local date; your answer to the notification prompt; whether a review prompt or the screen after a finish was shown and what you tapped there;
  • your plan status, whether reminders are allowed, and the day you installed the app;
  • device and app details: device model and type, iOS version, language, time zone, screen size, connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile), App Store country, and app version and build;
  • technical tags, such as the build channel and which consent setting applies.

Each event carries a random identifier created on your device when sharing starts. It is not your Apple Account or your advertising identifier.

Advertising (free version only)

From version 1.4, if you use the app without a plan, it shows ads from Google AdMob between patterns, as a small banner on some menu screens, and as optional videos you can choose to watch for extra play. To show, measure and protect these ads, Google's software in the app collects:

  • device identifiers: an identifier for this app on your device and, only if you allow tracking when iOS asks, your device's advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • your IP address and the approximate location (country or region) worked out from it;
  • device and app details, such as device model, iOS version, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads, such as views, clicks and whether a video was watched to the end;
  • performance and crash data from the ad software;
  • your privacy choices (consent, or opting out of sale or sharing), which are stored on your device.

Ads are limited to content that suits the app's age rating on the App Store. If you open the app with an active plan, the ad software isn't started; if you subscribe while using the app, ads stop at once and the ad software stays idle until you next open the app.

Ad settings file

The app downloads a small settings file from gyra.io (gyra.io/stitchwell/ads.json) that tells it whether ads are switched on. It sends no personal data with this request. As with any web request, our website host receives your IP address.

Data you choose to send us

  • Emails to support: your email address, your message and anything you attach.
  • Diagnostics you share: if the app has crashed, frozen or run slowly, you can send us the technical report that iOS gave it (Settings › About › Send diagnostics). It contains your device model, iOS and app version and the code that was running.

3. How we use data and our legal bases

  • Usage statistics are used to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Where the law requires consent, we rely on your consent; otherwise on our legitimate interest in improving the app.
  • Advertising. Personalised ads, and storing or reading information on your device for ads, rely on your consent where the law requires it (in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, through Google's consent form in the app) and on your permission in the iOS tracking prompt for your advertising identifier. Where no consent is given, Google may still show non-personalised or limited ads and use data to measure ads and prevent fraud, based on our legitimate interest in keeping a free version of the app. Ads never decide what you can stitch.
  • Support emails and diagnostics are used to answer you and fix problems you report, on the basis of our legitimate interest in helping you or to perform our contract with you.
  • Any of the above may be used where needed to meet legal obligations or to establish or defend legal claims.

You do not have to give us personal data to use the app. We do not make decisions about you by automated means.

4. Who receives data

  • PostHog Inc. and its sub-processors process the usage statistics for us, on servers in the EU, under a data processing agreement.
  • Google (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere) receives the advertising data described in section 2 and uses it as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. The consent form in the app lists Google's advertising partners that may also receive data. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • Our email provider stores support emails for us.
  • Authorities or courts, if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.
  • A successor, if Stitchwell is sold or transferred.

Each provider that processes data for us must protect it at least as well as this policy does. Apart from the advertising described above, we do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

5. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where the law requires it, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for a copy. Google transfers advertising data to the United States under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires, and then delete it. Your ad privacy choices stay on your device until you change them or delete the app. Google keeps advertising data according to its own retention periods (policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children

Stitchwell is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the higher age set by the law where you live). If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it. Ads in the app are limited to content that suits its age rating.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier use), and to complain to your data protection authority. If you are in Türkiye, you also have the rights in Article 11 of Law No. 6698 (KVKK), including to learn who received your data in Türkiye or abroad, to have them told of any correction or deletion, to object to a result reached only by automated analysis, and to claim compensation for unlawful processing.

To change your ad choices, open Settings › Purchases › Privacy choices in the app (shown where the law gives you that choice), or email us. To stop personalised ads based on your advertising identifier, turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track, or tracking for Stitchwell, in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. A plan removes ads entirely.

To stop usage statistics, switch off Settings › Your data › Share usage analytics. For any other request, email support@gyra.io. Because usage statistics are tied only to a random identifier, we may not be able to find the data that belongs to you.

10. United States residents

  • Advertising and your right to opt out. In the free version, Google's ad software may receive identifiers, IP address, approximate location and ad interactions, which some state laws treat as a "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. You can opt out at any time: in the app (Settings › Purchases › Privacy choices, shown where your state's law gives you this right), by emailing support@gyra.io, and by not allowing tracking in iOS. Apart from this, we do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • The categories of data we collect and why are described in section 2 and section 3. To review or delete your data, contact us as described in section 9.
  • Do Not Track: the app does not respond to Do Not Track signals. It uses your advertising identifier only if you allow tracking when iOS asks.
