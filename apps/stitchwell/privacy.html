Stitchwell Privacy Policy

Effective date: [OWNER: publication date]

This policy explains what personal data Stitchwell for iPhone and iPad (the "app") collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and what rights and choices you have. It applies only to the app. The gyra.io website has its own privacy page.

Summary of key points

  • No account, no ads, no tracking. You never give us your name or email to play. The app never asks to track you and never reads the advertising identifier.
  • Your work stays yours. Your progress, creations and settings are kept on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud, which we cannot access.
  • Photos stay on your device. A photo you turn into a pattern is processed on your device and never uploaded.
  • Usage statistics, your choice. The app can send statistics about how it is used, tied only to a random ID and never to your name or Apple Account, to our analytics provider in the EU. If your App Store country is in the EU or EEA, nothing is sent unless you switch it on; otherwise you can switch it off at any time.
  • Apple handles payments and Game Center. We never see your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. What data we collect

In short: only usage statistics tied to a random ID (if sharing is on), and whatever you choose to send us.

Usage statistics (pseudonymous, collected automatically if sharing is on)

When "Share usage analytics" is on (see Your choices), the app sends events about how it is used, for example:

  • patterns started, left and finished (the pattern's catalogue number, size and how far along it is), and how many patterns you have finished;
  • the subscription screen: when it is shown, which plan is chosen, and whether a free trial or purchase went through, renewed, was cancelled or was refunded (never your payment details or Apple Account);
  • steps of making a photo pattern, such as "photo loaded" or "pattern made", with the pattern's size in stitches and number of colours (never the photo or its name);
  • a few taps, such as opening a notification, sharing a finished piece and which export you shared (picture, time-lapse or PDF);
  • time spent in a pattern and your local date; your answer to the notification prompt; whether a review prompt or the screen after a finish was shown and what you tapped there;
  • your plan status, whether reminders are allowed, and the day you installed the app;
  • device and app details: device model and type, iOS version, language, time zone, screen size, connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile), App Store country, and app version and build;
  • technical tags we use to keep test builds out of our numbers, such as the build channel and which consent setting applies.

Each event carries a random identifier created on your device when sharing starts. It is not your Apple Account, your advertising identifier or your device's vendor identifier, and we never link it to your name, email or any other identifier. Our provider is set to discard your IP address and not to look up your location from it.

Data you choose to send us

  • Emails to support: your email address, your message and anything you attach.
  • Diagnostics you share: iOS may give the app technical reports when it crashes or freezes (for example, which part of the code was running). They stay on your device and reach us only if you send them yourself (Settings › About › Send diagnostics, shown once a report exists). They contain your device model, iOS and app version and the code that was running; no photos or patterns.

Data that stays with you

Your progress, finished pieces, photo patterns and settings are stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your private iCloud account (see Apple services). We cannot see or access them.

3. What we do not collect

  • Your name, email (unless you write to us), phone number or any account details.
  • Your photos, or the names you give patterns or photos. For a photo pattern we receive only its size and number of colours.
  • Anything you type, your contacts, or your precise location. We do not look up your location from your IP address; we receive only your App Store country and time zone.
  • Your advertising identifier, or any data for advertising. The app contains no advertising, attribution or tracking software, and records no screen recordings or automatic tap capture.
  • Payment card or bank details (Apple handles payments).
  • Sensitive data, such as health, biometric or religious information.

5. Who receives data

In short: our analytics and email providers, working for us. We never sell your data.

  • PostHog Inc. and its sub-processors (such as its EU cloud host) process the usage statistics for us, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (PostHog Cloud EU), under a data processing agreement that lets it use the data only to provide its service to us.
  • Our email provider stores support emails for us, as our processor.
  • Authorities or courts, if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.
  • A successor, if Stitchwell is sold or transferred. This policy would continue to protect your data, and we would tell you in the app first.

Each provider must protect your data at least as well as this policy does. We do not sell personal data, share it for advertising, or give it to data brokers. Apple provides its own services to you under its own privacy policy (see Apple services).

Any other transfer outside the EU, EEA or UK is protected by an adequacy decision or the Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards.

8. Your choices in the app

  • Usage statistics: Settings › Your data › Share usage analytics. If your App Store country is in the EU or EEA, it starts off; otherwise it starts on. Switching it off stops sending at once and deletes the random identifier from your device; switching it back on creates a new one. Statistics already sent stay with our provider until they are deleted under section 7. Withdrawing consent does not affect what was sent before.
  • Notifications: the first time you open the app, iOS asks whether Stitchwell may send notifications. If you allow it, a daily reminder at 7 pm is switched on. Reminders are created on your device and nothing is sent to us. Change the time or turn them off in Settings › Reminders, or in your device's Settings. A separate "New patterns" note (at most one a week) is off until you turn it on there.
  • Game Center: off until you turn it on in Settings › Play.
  • Your work: Settings › Your data lets you export your work, restore an earlier copy, or delete all progress. "Delete all progress" resets every pattern's progress on this device and in your iCloud; your achievements, photo patterns and the earlier copies on this device stay. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.
  • iCloud: turn sync off, or delete the app's iCloud data, in your device's Settings › your name › iCloud.

9. Apple services the app uses

These services are run by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not receive your personal data from them.

  • iCloud: if you are signed in, your progress syncs between your devices through your private iCloud database. We cannot see or access it.
  • Photo picker: you choose a photo with Apple's picker, so the app sees only the photo you pick, never your library. The photo is turned into a pattern on your device and never uploaded; only the pattern is saved, on your device and in your iCloud.
  • In-App Purchase: subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. The app reads your subscription status from Apple and, if sharing is on, sends the plan events described in section 2. Manage or cancel it in your device's Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • Game Center: if you turn it on, the app reports your achievements to Apple. It does not store or send your Game Center player ID. Stitchwell has no leaderboards.
  • App analytics from Apple: if you share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage totals. Change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data: data is encrypted in transit, analytics are stored with an EU provider bound by a data processing agreement, and access is limited to us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we will tell you and the authorities about a breach where the law requires it.

11. Children

Stitchwell is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the higher age of consent where you live). The usage statistics are used only to improve the app, never for advertising. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, for example in an email, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy;
  • have it corrected or deleted;
  • restrict or object to its use, including use based on our legitimate interest;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier use;
  • complain to your data protection authority (in the EU, where you live or work; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

How to use them: email support@gyra.io. It is free, and we answer within one month (we may extend this by two months for complex requests, and will tell you). The usage statistics are tied only to a random ID on your device. We hold nothing else that identifies you, so unless you can give us that ID we may not be able to find your data (GDPR Art. 11). Switching sharing off deletes the ID from your device, and data already sent is deleted by our provider within 1 year.
