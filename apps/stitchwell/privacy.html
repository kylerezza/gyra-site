Stitchwell Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Stitchwell for iPhone and iPad (the "app") collects, why, who receives it, how long it is kept and what rights you have. It applies only to the app. The gyra.io website has its own privacy page.

Summary of key points

  • No account, no ads, no tracking. You do not need to give us your name or email to use the app, and the app does not ask to track you.
  • Usage statistics. The app can send statistics about how it is used to our analytics provider. You can switch this off at any time.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.
  • We do not sell your personal data.

2. What data we collect

Usage statistics (collected automatically, if sharing is on)

When "Share usage analytics" is on, the app sends events about how it is used, for example:

  • patterns started, left and finished (the pattern's catalogue number, size and how far along it is), and how many patterns you have finished;
  • the subscription screen: when it is shown, which plan is chosen, and whether a free trial or purchase went through, renewed, was cancelled or was refunded;
  • steps of making a photo pattern, such as "photo loaded" or "pattern made", with the pattern's size in stitches and number of colours;
  • taps such as opening a notification, sharing a finished piece and which export you shared;
  • time spent in a pattern and your local date; your answer to the notification prompt; whether a review prompt or the screen after a finish was shown and what you tapped there;
  • your plan status, whether reminders are allowed, and the day you installed the app;
  • device and app details: device model and type, iOS version, language, time zone, screen size, connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile), App Store country, and app version and build;
  • technical tags, such as the build channel and which consent setting applies.

Each event carries a random identifier created on your device when sharing starts. It is not your Apple Account or your advertising identifier.

Data you choose to send us

  • Emails to support: your email address, your message and anything you attach.
  • Diagnostics you share: if the app has crashed or frozen, you can send us the technical report that iOS gave it (Settings › About › Send diagnostics). It contains your device model, iOS and app version and the code that was running.

3. How we use data and our legal bases

  • Usage statistics are used to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Where the law requires consent, we rely on your consent; otherwise on our legitimate interest in improving the app.
  • Support emails and diagnostics are used to answer you and fix problems you report, on the basis of our legitimate interest in helping you or to perform our contract with you.
  • Any of the above may be used where needed to meet legal obligations or to establish or defend legal claims.

You do not have to give us personal data to use the app. We do not make decisions about you by automated means.

4. Who receives data

  • PostHog Inc. and its sub-processors process the usage statistics for us, on servers in the EU, under a data processing agreement.
  • Our email provider stores support emails for us.
  • Authorities or courts, if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or someone's safety.
  • A successor, if Stitchwell is sold or transferred.

Each provider that processes data for us must protect it at least as well as this policy does. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.

5. International transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. Where the law requires it, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above, or as long as the law requires, and then delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children

Stitchwell is made for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the higher age set by the law where you live). If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable format, to withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier use), and to complain to your data protection authority.

To stop usage statistics, switch off Settings › Your data › Share usage analytics. For any other request, email support@gyra.io. Because usage statistics are tied only to a random identifier, we may not be able to find the data that belongs to you.

10. United States residents

  • We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • The categories of data we collect and why are described in section 2 and section 3. To review or delete your data, contact us as described in section 9.
  • Do Not Track: the app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, so it does not respond to Do Not Track signals.

11. Changes to this policy
