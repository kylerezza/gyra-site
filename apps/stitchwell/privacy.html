Stitchwell Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026

This policy covers Stitchwell for iPhone and iPad, made by gyra ("gyra", "we", "us"). gyra is the trading name of the individual developer shown as the seller on Stitchwell's App Store page.

The short version

Stitchwell has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. To learn how the app is used and make it better, it sends anonymous usage statistics (for example "a pattern was finished" or "the plan screen was opened") to our analytics provider, PostHog, on servers in the EU. These never include your name, your photos, pattern names or anything you type, and you can switch them off at any time in Settings › Your data › Share usage analytics. Your progress stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud. Purchases and Game Center are handled by Apple.

No ads, no tracking

The current version of Stitchwell has no ads and contains no advertising, attribution or crash-reporting software. It never asks to track you and never reads the advertising identifier. We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising, and the usage statistics described below are never given to advertisers or data brokers or combined with advertising data.

Not in the current version: a later update may add ads. If it does, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy label before that version is released, describe exactly which data the ad provider collects, and explain your choices (including the tracking prompt and a "Do Not Sell or Share" option).

Anonymous usage statistics

What is sent. Events about how the app is used: when a pattern is started, left or finished (its catalogue number, size band and how far along it is), when the plan screen is shown and what happens on it (which plan was chosen, whether a trial or purchase went through, a later renewal, cancellation or refund), steps of making a photo pattern (for example "photo loaded" or "pattern made", with its size in stitches, never the photo or its name), notification taps, share taps, and device and app details: device model and type, iOS version, language, time zone, screen size, connection type (Wi-Fi or mobile), App Store country, app version and build, whether reminders are on, whether ads are on, and the day you installed the app. Each event carries a random identifier created on your device when sharing starts (the first time the app runs, or when you switch sharing on). It is not your Apple Account, the advertising identifier or the device's vendor identifier, and it is not linked to your name or email.

What is never sent. Your photos or anything derived from them, pattern or photo names, anything you type, your contacts, precise location, or your IP address (our provider is set to discard it and not to look up your location from it). There is no screen recording and no automatic capture of taps.

Why and on what basis. To understand which patterns and features people use, where they get stuck, and whether changes to the app help, so we can improve it. In the European Union and EEA we rely on your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)): nothing is sent until you switch "Share usage analytics" on in Settings › Your data. Elsewhere, including the United Kingdom, Switzerland, the United States and Australia, the switch starts on and you can turn it off at any time; in the UK this is statistical analysis to improve the service, and our legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the app (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).

Who receives it. PostHog Inc., our analytics processor, under a data processing agreement, on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (PostHog Cloud EU). PostHog uses the data only to provide the service to us. PostHog Inc. is a US company; any access from outside the EU is covered by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses in that agreement.

How long. Usage events are kept up to 1 year, then deleted. We may keep monthly totals (for example "finishes per week") that contain no identifiers.

Your choice. Settings › Your data › Share usage analytics. Switching it off stops sending at once and deletes the random identifier from your device; switching it back on starts a new one. You can also ask us to delete data already sent (see Your rights); because we do not know who you are, tell us roughly when you used the app and on which device, and we will do our best to find it.

Data we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email or any account. Apart from the anonymous usage statistics above, the app sends nothing to us or to anyone else.

Where your game data lives

On your device

Your progress, settings and creations are stored on your device.

Your own iCloud (optional)

If you are signed in to iCloud, your progress can sync between your devices through Apple's iCloud, in your private iCloud account. We cannot see or access this data. You can turn this off and delete it in Settings › your name › iCloud.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Your game data stays on your device and in your iCloud until you delete it. Settings › Delete all progress in the app resets the progress of every pattern, here and in your iCloud (your achievements, your photo patterns and the earlier copies on this device stay). Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device; data in your iCloud can be deleted in Settings › your name › iCloud.

Photos you choose

The app lets you turn a photo into a pattern. You pick the photo with Apple's photo picker, so the app only sees the photo you choose and never your whole library. The photo is processed on your device and is never uploaded. Only the finished pattern is saved, on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account.

Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email or payment details. The app only asks Apple whether a purchase or subscription is active. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Game Center (optional)

Game Center is off until you turn it on in the app's settings. If you do, the app reports your achievements to Apple, which handles them under Apple's Privacy Policy. The app does not store or send your Game Center player ID. Stitchwell has no leaderboards.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created on your device. They are off until you turn them on.

Diagnostics you choose to send

If the app crashes or freezes, iOS may give it a technical report (for example, which part of the code was running). The app keeps these reports on your device only. They leave it only if you choose to send them (Settings › About › Send diagnostics): that opens the share sheet, and you decide where they go, for example an email to us. They contain no photos, patterns or personal details.

Crash reports from Apple

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. Change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Stitchwell is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children; the anonymous usage statistics are used only to run and improve the app, never for advertising. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, delete, correct or object to the use of personal data, to withdraw consent at any time (which does not affect use before you withdrew it), and to complain to your data protection authority. The only data we hold are the anonymous usage statistics above, which we cannot tie to your name, and emails you choose to send us. Write to us and we will answer any request, including deleting your emails. Residents of California and other US states: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Emails to support

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and keep them only as long as we need to answer and follow up. We never add you to a mailing list or share your email.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above, and update the App Store privacy label before the changed version is released.
