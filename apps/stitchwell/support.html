Stitchwell support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email support@gyra.io. We usually reply within 2 business days.

Tell us your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and what happened. A screenshot helps. Never send card details.

Playing

Where are the settings?

Tap the gear on the Today screen. Sounds, music, haptics, reminders, Game Center and your data are all there.

I made a mistake on a pattern.

Every pattern keeps an earlier copy every 10 minutes of stitching. Go to Settings › Restore an earlier copy and pick the one you want.

How do I turn a photo into a pattern?

On the Patterns tab, tap Turn a photo into a pattern and choose a photo. You only share the photo you pick, it's turned into a pattern on your device, and only the pattern is saved. Your first photo pattern is free.

Purchases

What does the monthly plan include?

Every pattern in the library, past Daily Hoops and unlimited photo patterns while it's active. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. The price in your currency is shown before you confirm.

What happens when the plan ends?

Patterns you started while paying for the plan stay open. Patterns you started during a free trial that ends without a payment close again until you subscribe.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel?

In Settings › Manage subscription, or on your device: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Stitchwell. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app doesn't cancel it.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and choose the Stitchwell purchase.

Your progress

My progress isn't on my new device.

Stitchwell syncs through your own iCloud. Make sure both devices are signed in to the same iCloud account with iCloud on for Stitchwell (Settings › your name › iCloud), and open the app once on the old device while online.

How do I delete my progress?

Go to Settings › Delete all progress and confirm. This resets the progress of every pattern, here and in your iCloud. Your photo patterns, achievements and the earlier copies on this device stay. It can't be undone, and it doesn't cancel a subscription.

The app crashed.

Go to Settings › About › Send diagnostics and send the reports to us. They contain no photos, patterns or personal details.
